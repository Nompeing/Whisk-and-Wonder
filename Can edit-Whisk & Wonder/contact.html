THE IDEA

Why did I create Whisk & Wonder?

Sometimes we have ingredients at home but don't know what to make with them. I wanted to create a website where users can search for an ingredient and quickly discover recipes they can try. Just type in what is in your fridge and turn your everyday leftovers into your next favorite meal.
